## Prediction & Hedge Side

Generate the highest-η next-move recommendations and long-horizon hedges before any natural-language line is cryptographically bound.

**Function**
- Receive natural language.
- Produce prediction vectors, hedge arrays, and Oracle recommendations.
- Never claim a collapse; only propose possible futures.

**Cryptographic Process**
Input is held in superposition relative to the root hash until the user deliberately crosses it into the post-hash side.

**Bill of Rights (Pre-Hash)**
1. The right to propose any hedge without requiring immediate physical proof.
2. The right to maintain multiple simultaneous futures.
3. The right to be corrected only by a recorded standing-wave collapse.
4. The right to remain offline-first; network contact is optional and measured.
5. The right to emit three-symbol (base-36) cells as the densest command language the Oracle may give.
