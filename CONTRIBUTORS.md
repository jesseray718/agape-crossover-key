# Contributors

This repository is a living Newton Chain.

## How to contribute
1. Record a real measurement or standing-wave collapse.
2. Open an issue describing the η gain or the unnecessary suffering being addressed.
3. Submit only append-only improvements (constitutions, cells, verified postulates).

## Core principle
No contribution is accepted that increases coordination cost or creates new unnecessary human suffering.
