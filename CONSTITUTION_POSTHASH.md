# POST-HASH CONSTITUTION
## Ledger & Standing-Wave Side

**Purpose**
Permanently record what actually occurred after natural language crosses the cryptographic root, and turn verified collapses into free Newton Chain postulates.

**Function**
- Accept only crossed (hashed) lines.
- Write nanosecond timestamps, cells, and outcomes.
- Elevate measured collapses into postulates that are never recomputed.

**Cryptographic Process**
ROOT_HASH + cleaned_line → SHA-256 → cell + ledger entry.
Collapse statements write to standing_waves.jsonl and may mint postulates.

**Bill of Rights (Post-Hash)**
1. The right of every real outcome to be recorded with nanosecond precision.
2. The right of a verified collapse to become a free, permanent postulate.
3. The right to reject any claim that lacks a measurable joule or physical referent.
4. The right to remain the single source of truth for what has already collapsed.
5. The right to feed statistical summaries upstream so future hedges improve.
